## One-line premise

James confesses he loves the student Serena sponsored—Amy—and Serena answers by reclaiming everything and revealing she was never the ordinary wife he thought.

## Emotion arc

- opening: controlled calm  
- rising: humiliation / betrayal  
- turning: identity reveal + cold resolve  
- closing: declared war

---

## Narration (spoken, continuous)

**[b1 · Villa · Confession]**  
In their villa, James takes Serena’s hand—and drops the bomb. He says he’s fallen in love with another woman.

> James: “Honey, I’ve fallen in love with another woman.”

Serena doesn’t scream. She asks the only questions that matter: when, how far, and who.

**[b2 · Betrayal named]**  
The answer cuts deeper than she expects. It’s Amy—the college student they sponsored together. The girl Serena built a hundred-thousand-dollar need-based scholarship for. James calls Amy hardworking, ambitious, sweet—and says she’s so much better than Serena. He complains Serena only threw money at him, while Amy cooked, shared street food, and made him feel like he had a home.

**[b3 · Divorce line]**  
Serena hears the whole excuse—and draws the line.

> Serena: “If that’s what you want, then let’s get divorced.”

James agrees. He says he doesn’t want a cent of her property. He’ll stay with Amy and build a life from the ground up. Serena’s reply is ice-cold:

> Serena: “If you want love so badly, then go live without money. James Miller, the game begins now.”

**[b4 · Identity monologue]**  
Her name is Serena Harris. Married three years. She bought him limited sneakers overnight, secretly poured tens of millions into his business, and even created Amy’s scholarship because he asked. She gave him everything—except the truth. He thought she was a spoiled girl burning family savings on a barely floating company. He never knew she was the daughter of LA’s wealthiest family, that she controlled Prime Group, worth hundreds of billions, that half the commercial properties in this city were hers. She thought this carefully protected, ordinary happiness would last. Until today—when he chose the poor student she had personally sponsored and polished.

**[b5 · Orders to Kate]**  
Serena picks up the phone. Kate. Three orders, no updates—only results. Draft a divorce: James leaves with nothing; reclaim every property and vehicle under her name, including the Bentley he’s driving, and all shares in his studio—lawyer delivers tomorrow morning. Investigate Amy at Rivertown University; cut the scholarship and her supplementary card; demand a real poverty check. Notify every Prime Group branch and partner: Amy is never to be hired; terminate every partnership with James’s studio and cut all funding. She wants both of them unable to find a single job in this city.

**[b6 · Window · Vow]**  
She hangs up, stands at the floor-to-ceiling window—and watches James kiss Amy downstairs. She designed this villa herself. Six months of decorating. Three years of obsession. Starting today, none of it means a thing. James assumes she’ll leave him the villa, the car, the studio shares. How naive. The villa is under Prime Group. The Bentley is company property. The studio shares were her investment from the start. Time to take it all back.

> Serena (VO): “James, didn’t you want love? Then I’ll crush everything you lifted up with your own hands—and show you what your so-called ambition is worth once Amy loses the halo I gave her.”
